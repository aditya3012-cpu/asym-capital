About ASYM Capital

ASYM Capital is both a proprietary trading firm and a Quantitative-as-a-Service (QaaS) provider. We trade our own capital systematically, and we provide the same research, execution and risk infrastructure to clients.

Founded in 2024, ASYM Capital was built on a single thesis: the highest-quality opportunities in any market are asymmetric. They are positions where the potential upside materially outweighs the downside, where the distribution of outcomes is skewed in your favour, and where disciplined modelling and execution turn that skew into a repeatable edge.

We build and run end-to-end systematic trading infrastructure, from signal research and portfolio construction through to live order execution and risk control. The systems that trade our own book are the same ones we deploy for clients, at any portfolio size.

We work with a select group of institutions, family offices, funds and high-net-worth investors, and we bring to every engagement the same rigour we apply to our own capital.

Our Philosophy

“In markets, symmetry is overrated. The best opportunities are not balanced; they are skewed. We find them, measure them and execute against them systematically.”

The word asymmetric is not decoration. It is the foundation of every model we build, every position we take and every system we architect. We are in the business of identifying probability distributions skewed in our favour, and acting on them with precision.

This means rejecting “balanced” portfolio construction when the data shows directional skew. It means stress-testing the tail, not just the mean. It means building execution algorithms that preserve edge rather than erode it. And it means bringing that same philosophy to every client engagement we take on.

Rigour, not intuition. Evidence, not narrative. Asymmetry, not symmetry.
