About

Founded in 2022 on a single thesis: the highest-quality opportunities in any market are asymmetric.

They are positions where the potential upside materially outweighs the downside, where the distribution of outcomes is skewed in your favour, and where disciplined modelling and execution turn that skew into a repeatable edge.

Proprietary trading

We trade our own capital on end-to-end systematic infrastructure, from signal research and portfolio construction through to live order execution and risk control.

Philosophy

In markets, symmetry is overrated. The best opportunities are not balanced; they are skewed.

We find those opportunities, measure them and execute against them systematically. Every model we build and every position we take starts from the same question: is the distribution of outcomes skewed in our favour?

In practice, that means rejecting “balanced” portfolio construction when the data shows directional skew, stress-testing the tail rather than just the mean, and building execution algorithms that preserve edge rather than erode it. Clients get the same discipline.
